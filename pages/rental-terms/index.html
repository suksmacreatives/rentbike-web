01

Booking & payment

How do I confirm a booking?

Contact Juli Rentbike with your preferred vehicle, rental dates, and pickup details. Your booking is confirmed only after the availability and booking arrangements have been agreed upon with our team.

What payment methods are accepted?

Payment methods, payment deadlines, and any deposit requirements will be communicated by our team when your booking is arranged.

Are the displayed rental prices final?

Confirm the final rental price before booking. Delivery, additional services, deposits, and other agreed charges may affect the total amount payable.

02

Rental requirements

What documents do I need?

Required documents depend on your vehicle, age, residency, and applicable regulations. Ask our team which identification and valid driving licence documents you must provide before pickup.

Can international visitors rent a motorcycle?

International visitors must meet the applicable driving licence and legal requirements for the vehicle they intend to operate. Please verify your eligibility before confirming your rental.

Can someone else drive the rented vehicle?

Only drivers approved under the rental arrangement and legally qualified to operate the vehicle should drive it. Confirm any additional-driver conditions with our team in advance.

03

Pickup & return

Where can I collect the vehicle?

The pickup location must be agreed upon during booking. If delivery is requested, confirm the destination, delivery time, and any applicable additional fee with our team.

What should I check before leaving?

Check the vehicle's general condition, lights, brakes, tyres, fuel level, and any provided equipment. Document existing damage and report concerns to the rental team before departure.

What happens if I return the vehicle late?

Contact our team as soon as you expect a delay. Any grace period, additional rental charge, or change to the return arrangement must be confirmed with the rental team.

04

Vehicle responsibility

How should I care for the vehicle?

Use the vehicle responsibly, follow traffic rules, park securely, and take reasonable precautions against theft or damage. Do not use the vehicle for illegal activities or unauthorised purposes.

Is wearing a helmet required?

Motorcycle riders and passengers must follow applicable helmet and road-safety requirements. Confirm helmet availability and condition before starting your journey.

Can I take the vehicle outside Bali?

Do not take the vehicle outside the agreed rental area without obtaining prior approval from Juli Rentbike.

05

Damage & accidents

What should I do if an accident occurs?

Prioritise everyone's safety and seek emergency assistance when necessary. Notify Juli Rentbike as soon as possible, document the incident when safe, and follow applicable legal requirements.

Who is responsible for vehicle damage?

Responsibility for damage, loss, repair costs, and any insurance coverage depends on the circumstances and the agreed rental terms. Ask for the applicable conditions before accepting the vehicle.

What if the vehicle develops a mechanical problem?

Stop in a safe location and contact our team for assistance. Do not attempt major repairs or arrange expensive repairs without authorisation, except where immediate action is necessary for safety.

06

Cancellation & extensions

Can I cancel my booking?

Contact our team as early as possible if you need to cancel. Any refund, cancellation charge, or deposit arrangement will follow the policy agreed upon when the booking was confirmed.

Can I extend my rental period?

Rental extensions are subject to vehicle availability and approval. Contact our team before your original return time to confirm the extended period and any additional charges.

What if my travel plans change?

Let our team know as soon as your plans change. We can discuss available options based on the original booking agreement and vehicle availability.
